Sustainability & Assurance

We help organisations prepare sustainability information and coordinate with qualified third parties. Smart Focus Solution does not itself provide independent assurance or certification.

  • Consulting: sustainability and ESG advice
  • Reporting support: data collection and reporting processes, performance monitoring, better documentation
  • Assurance coordination: liaising with qualified third-party providers
  • Independent assurance: performed only by a qualified third party

Value: more reliable, better-documented sustainability disclosures.
